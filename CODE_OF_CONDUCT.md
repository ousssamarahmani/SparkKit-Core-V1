# SparkKit Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and specific when giving feedback.
- Discuss technical decisions without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Accept maintainer decisions while remaining free to disagree constructively.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, and publishing private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Reports should be made privately using the contact process in `SECURITY.md`; clearly label conduct reports so they are routed appropriately.

Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical and will consider context, severity, history, and community safety when deciding a response.
