# Acknowledgements

SparkKit is built and maintained by people. GitHub's commit and pull-request
history remains the authoritative record of human authorship and contribution.

## AI development contributors

The project also acknowledges the coding assistants used under maintainer
direction:

- **Codex by OpenAI** — assisted with architecture analysis, implementation,
  testing, documentation, and repository maintenance.
- **Claude Code by Anthropic** — assisted with implementation, review, and
  development workflows.

AI assistants are development tools, not project maintainers, legal authors,
copyright holders, or substitutes for human review. A human maintainer remains
responsible for accepting changes, validating behavior, and protecting the
project's security and tenancy boundaries.
